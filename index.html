    How to play
    
      
      
    • Take turns with Red Pen drawing one line between two dots. Close a box and it's yours, and you go again.
      • 
      
    • Each box you win shows its letter in the hidden answer. Boxes Red Pen wins keep their letter hidden.
      • 
      
    • A different game each day, getting harder through the week: Monday phrase · Tuesday landmark and city · Wednesday city and country · Thursday phrase · Friday song and artist, or film and actor on alternate weeks (6×6 board) · Saturday hard phrase · Sunday word day.
      • 
      
    • You start with no clues. Some boxes hide a clue (marked ?): win the box to read it free, or buy it for 50 (tap twice). If you then win its box anyway, you get the 50 back.
      • 
      
    • Stuck? Show best move costs 50. Skip my turn makes Red Pen move instead for 100.
      • 
      
    • Once the board is full, tap a red +25 box to buy its letter.
      • 
      
    • Guess the answer on your turn whenever you think you've got it. You get 3 guesses. Score: 100 for solving, +20 for every letter still unclaimed, +5 per box, plus a speed bonus (under 1 min +150, 2 min +100, 3 min +50, 4 min +25, 5 min +10).
      • 
      
    • Sunday word day: every box has a letter. Win as many as you can, then you get up to 5 minutes to make words from your letters (3 letters or more, each letter once per word). Letters have Scrabble values (shown in the corner): most are 1, but J and X are 8, Q and Z are 10. A word scores its letter values × 5, plus a length bonus: 3 letters +10, 4 +20, 5 +35, 6 +55, 7 +80, 8 or more +110 and up.
      • 
      
    • Later days are worth more (×1 on Monday up to ×3 on Sunday). Monday to Sunday adds up to a weekly total, then it starts again.
      • 
      
    • Everyone gets the same board and Red Pen plays the same way, so scores are comparable.
      • 
      
    • Tap the 🏆 to pick a nickname and get on the leaderboard: today, this week, last week and all time. Your daily score is saved when you finish.
